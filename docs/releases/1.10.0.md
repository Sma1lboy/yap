# Yap 1.10.0

Changes since 1.9.0. 中文在下面。

Yap is an open-source (GPL-3.0) dictation app for macOS, forked from VoiceInk. Hold a shortcut, talk in English, Chinese or both in one sentence, and let go: Yap transcribes, cleans up, and pastes. Run it on a local model, with your own provider key, or through Yap Cloud.

## Meetings

- Yap now notices calls. When Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Slack, Discord, FaceTime, Skype, Feishu, Lark, Tencent Meeting, VooV Meeting, DingTalk or a browser (Chrome, Safari, Arc, Edge, Firefox, Brave) has used the microphone for 5 seconds, a notification asks "Zoom is on a call. Record this meeting?" with a Record Meeting button. For a browser it says "Google Chrome is using the microphone. Record this meeting?", since Yap can't tell that a browser is in a meeting. It asks once per call; a call counts as over once the app has been off the microphone for 10 seconds.
- While a meeting records, once every call app that was on the microphone has been off it for 10 seconds, a notification says "The call seems to have ended. Click ✓ to end the meeting and save it." with a Show Meeting Panel button.
- Yap never starts or stops a recording by itself: only ✓ in the meeting panel ends a meeting. Neither notification appears while you dictate or while another notification is on screen; they wait until then.
- The switch is "Remind Me to Record When a Call Starts" in the new Settings › Meetings section. It is on if you have recorded a meeting before and off if you only dictate. Settings › Meetings also holds the Record Meeting shortcut, which moved out of Additional Shortcuts.
- Without headphones, the other side's voice no longer shows up a second time under "Me". After the meeting, Yap compares your microphone with the other apps' sound, and lines or parts of lines that are the other side coming out of your speakers are taken out of the transcript, the notes and the Markdown export. The panel says "Removed N echoes: your microphone picked up the other side from the speakers." With headphones nothing is changed.
- A long meeting no longer waits for its speakers to be told apart. If that isn't done 10 seconds after you click ✓, the meeting is saved with plain "Others", the notes are written and the result is shown. The panel and the History row show "Telling speakers apart…"; when it's done the transcript gets "Others 1", "Others 2"…, and the panel tells you to click Regenerate Notes if you want the notes to use them. If Yap quits first, it finishes at the next launch. Measured on an Apple M4 Pro with the speaker model already downloaded, telling speakers apart takes 31 to 36 seconds for an hour of meeting.
- When telling speakers apart fails, the History row shows "Speakers not told apart" with the reason as its tooltip.
- History showed a meeting's transcript as one run-on paragraph, with each timestamped line running into the next. It now shows one line per piece.
- A meeting in History has the tabs Transcript and Notes instead of Original and Enhanced, and its row has a "Meeting" tag. In Chinese the notes heading of the Markdown export now says 纪要 (it said 备忘录).
- The result panel lists its status lines in one order: errors first, then warnings, then information. A speaker split that failed or timed out is now a warning, not grey text.
- For a meeting where nothing was said, the panel shows "(Nothing was said in this meeting.)" without Copy Transcript or Regenerate Notes, and its History row has no Regenerate Notes or Speaker Names….
- When a meeting couldn't be saved, the panel no longer offers Open History. A failed Markdown export no longer shows the meeting folder and Show in Finder.
- When the notes can't be written, the panel says "The notes couldn't be written: …" instead of "Enhancement failed: …".
- In German and French, the recording panel's title and the consent note's buttons are no longer cut off.

## Notifications

- Notifications longer than one line were cut off after the first line, which hid the reason a recovered meeting couldn't be saved and the end of the German and French call notifications. Every notification now wraps to up to three lines.

## Known limits

- Call detection hasn't been tried in a real call yet. Most of the listed apps' IDs were taken from published lists, not from installed apps.
- Any use of the microphone by a browser, Slack or Discord looks like a call, so you may be asked when you aren't in a meeting. An app that lets go of the microphone while you're muted can make a long mute look like the end of the call.
- Echo is taken out after the meeting, from the recording. When you talk over the other side, some of your own words in that stretch can be cut, and echo that was transcribed differently on each side can stay.
- Yap doesn't turn on macOS echo cancellation for meetings: it would change how your microphone sounds and lower other apps' audio for the whole meeting.
- Speaker names belong to one meeting. The names you give in one meeting are not carried to the next.

# Yap 1.10.0（中文）

1.9.0 之后的改动。

Yap 是一个开源（GPL-3.0）的 macOS 听写 app，fork 自 VoiceInk。按住快捷键，用中文、英文或者一句话里中英混着说，松开：Yap 转写、清理，然后粘贴。可以用本机模型、你自己的服务商 key，或者 Yap Cloud。

## 会议

- Yap 现在能发现通话。Zoom、Microsoft Teams、Webex、Slack、Discord、FaceTime、Skype、飞书、Lark、腾讯会议、VooV Meeting、钉钉或浏览器（Chrome、Safari、Arc、Edge、Firefox、Brave）用麦克风满 5 秒时，会弹通知“Zoom 正在通话。要录这场会议吗？”，带“录制会议”按钮。浏览器的通知写的是“Google Chrome 正在使用麦克风。要录这场会议吗？”，因为 Yap 看不出浏览器是不是在开会。每次通话只问一次；这个 app 离开麦克风满 10 秒，才算这次通话结束。
- 会议录制中，开始时在用麦克风的通话 app 全都离开麦克风满 10 秒后，会弹通知“通话好像结束了。点 ✓ 结束会议并保存。”，带“显示会议面板”按钮。
- Yap 从不自己开始或停止录制：结束会议只能点会议面板上的 ✓。听写进行中、或者屏幕上已经有别的通知时，这两条通知都不弹，等到之后再弹。
- 开关是设置里新的“会议”分组中的“检测到通话时提醒我录制”。录过会议的用户默认打开，只听写的用户默认关闭。“录制会议”快捷键也从“其他快捷键”挪到了“会议”分组。
- 不戴耳机时，对方的声音不会再在“我”下面重复出现一遍。会议结束后，Yap 对比麦克风和其他 app 的声音，把扬声器外放进麦克风的对方的话从转写、纪要和导出的 Markdown 里去掉，整行或行里的一部分。面板上会写“去掉了 N 段扬声器回声：麦克风录进了外放的对方声音。”戴耳机时什么都不改。
- 长会议不再等说话人区分完。点 ✓ 后 10 秒还没区分完，会议先按“对方”保存，写好纪要，显示结果。面板和历史记录这一行显示“正在区分对方的说话人…”；区分完后转写里会标出“对方 1”“对方 2”…，面板会提示想让纪要也用上这些标签就点“重新生成纪要”。区分完之前 Yap 退出了，下次启动会接着做。在 Apple M4 Pro 上实测（说话人模型已下载好），一小时的会议区分说话人要 31 到 36 秒。
- 区分说话人失败时，历史记录这一行显示“未区分说话人”，鼠标悬停显示原因。
- 历史记录里会议的转写以前挤成一段，每行带时间的发言连在一起。现在一行一句。
- 历史记录里会议的两个标签页叫“转写”和“纪要”，不再是“原文”和“已润色”；这一行上有“会议”标签。导出 Markdown 里纪要的标题也改成了“纪要”（以前是“备忘录”）。
- 结果面板上的状态行按一个顺序排：先错误，再警告，最后是说明。区分说话人失败或超时现在算警告，不再是灰字。
- 会议里没说话时，面板显示“（这场会议里没有识别到说话内容。）”，不提供“复制转写”和“重新生成纪要”；历史记录里这一行也没有“重新生成纪要”和“说话人名字…”。
- 会议没能存进历史记录时，面板不再提供“打开历史记录”。导出 Markdown 失败时，不再显示会议文件夹和“在 Finder 中显示”。
- 纪要没能生成时，面板写“没能生成纪要：…”，不再是“润色失败：…”。
- 德语和法语界面下，录制面板的标题和录制告知的按钮不再被截断。

## 通知

- 超过一行的应用内通知以前只显示第一行：恢复会议没能保存的原因、德语和法语的通话提示后半截都看不到。现在所有通知最多换行显示三行。

## 已知限制

- 通话检测还没在真实通话里试过。列表里大部分 app 的标识来自公开资料，不是从装好的 app 里读的。
- 浏览器、Slack 或 Discord 只要用了麦克风就会被当成通话，所以没在开会时也可能被问。静音时会放开麦克风的 app，长时间静音可能被当成通话结束。
- 回声是会议结束后从录音里去掉的。你和对方同时说话时，那一段你自己的话可能被裁掉一部分；两边转写得不一样的回声可能留下。
- Yap 没有给会议打开 macOS 的回声消除：它会改变麦克风的声音，还会在整场会议期间压低其他 app 的音量。
- 说话人名字只在这一场会议里有效，不会带到下一场会议。
