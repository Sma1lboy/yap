# Yap 1.6.0

Changes since 1.5.0. 中文在下面。

Yap is an open-source (GPL-3.0) dictation app for macOS, forked from VoiceInk. Hold a shortcut, talk in English, Chinese or both in one sentence, and let go: Yap transcribes, cleans up, and pastes. Run it on a local model, with your own provider key, or through Yap Cloud.

## Language

- Yap's interface is now available in Traditional Chinese (繁體中文, with Taiwan terms), alongside English, German, French and Simplified Chinese. Pick it in the app language setting.

## Accessibility

- With Reduce Motion on, the recorder shows steady level bars, with no spinner, dot chase or spring animation.
- With Increase Contrast on, borders, secondary text and selections get higher-contrast colors.
- With VoiceOver on, Yap announces the dictation state as it changes: Recording, Transcribing, Enhancing, Pasted, and the title of a failure.

## Local models

- New Models › Advanced › Keep model loaded: After Each Dictation (default), 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 1 hour, or Always. A local model stays in memory for that long after a dictation.
- Under memory pressure a model is released sooner, but never during a recording or a transcription.
- Pressing the shortcut starts loading the model while you begin to speak.

# Yap 1.6.0（中文）

1.5.0 之后的改动。

Yap 是一个开源（GPL-3.0）的 macOS 听写 app，fork 自 VoiceInk。按住快捷键，用中文、英文或者一句话里中英混着说，松开：Yap 转写、清理，然后粘贴。可以用本机模型、你自己的服务商 key，或者 Yap Cloud。

## 语言

- Yap 的界面现在有繁体中文（繁體中文，用台湾的用词），和英文、德文、法文、简体中文并列。在 app 语言设置里选择。

## 辅助功能

- 开启“减少动态效果”时，录音条的音量条保持平稳，没有转圈、圆点追逐和弹簧动画。
- 开启“提高对比度”时，边框、次要文字和选中状态使用对比度更高的颜色。
- 开启旁白（VoiceOver）时，Yap 会在听写状态变化时播报：正在录音、正在转写、正在润色、已粘贴，以及失败的标题。

## 本地模型

- 新增 模型 › 高级 › 保持模型载入：每次听写后（默认）、5 分钟、15 分钟、1 小时或始终。听写结束后，本地模型会在内存里保留这么久。
- 内存吃紧时模型会更早释放，但录音和转写过程中不会释放。
- 按下快捷键时就开始载入模型，和你开口说话同时进行。
