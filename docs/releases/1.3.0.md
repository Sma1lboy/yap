# Yap 1.3.0

Changes since 1.2.0. 中文在下面。

Yap is an open-source (GPL-3.0) dictation app for macOS, forked from VoiceInk. Hold a shortcut, talk in English, Chinese or both in one sentence, and let go: Yap transcribes, cleans up, and pastes. Run it on a local model, with your own provider key, or through Yap Cloud.

## Signed and notarized by Apple

Yap is now signed with a Developer ID and notarized, so a zip downloaded in a browser opens with the usual "downloaded from the internet" prompt instead of being blocked. Because the signature changed, macOS asks for permissions once more after this update:

- Microphone: allow it again on your next recording.
- Accessibility: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility still shows Yap as on but it won't paste until you remove Yap from the list (−) and add it again, or toggle it off and on.
- Keychain: macOS asks once whether Yap may use its saved API key or Yap Cloud sign-in; choose Always Allow.

Later updates keep these permissions.

## Subtitles from audio files

- Transcribe an audio or video file with a local Whisper model, then Export Subtitles as SRT, WebVTT or Markdown with timestamps, from the transcription page or from History.
- Timestamps are in the whole file's time, including long files that are decoded in windows and recordings that switch language.

## Your dictionary reaches cloud transcription

- With OpenRouter or Yap Cloud, the words in your Dictionary are now sent to the transcription model, not only to cleanup, in the field each model actually reads. On the Chinese–English test set, the default model (mai-transcribe-2) got 68 of 82 key terms right instead of 59.
- Also sent for gpt-4o-transcribe, gpt-4o-mini-transcribe and whisper-large-v3. Models that ignore it (qwen3-asr-flash, gemini) don't get it.
- The transcription language you picked is sent too; with automatic detection nothing changes.

## Home, Dictionary and Settings

- Home shows your shortcuts for dictating, Undo Last Paste and Rewrite Last Dictation, with Change in Settings; its stat tiles are cards like the rest of the page.
- Dictionary lists can be filtered to Auto-added or Manually added words, and Select lets you delete many at once.
- Undo Last Paste and Rewrite Last Dictation have their own Voice Edits group in Settings. The four Chinese Cleanup options are switches with an explanation each.
- Help › Explore Key Features shows one card per feature with its current shortcut, and Show Me takes you to where it's set up.

## Also in 1.2.0, but missing from its notes

These shipped in 1.2.0 without being listed:

- **Meeting recording.** Right ⌘ + Space (change it under Settings › Shortcuts › Record Meeting) records your microphone and the sound from other apps until you press it again. Yap transcribes while the meeting goes on, then writes notes (summary, decisions, action items, open questions) with your mode's AI provider. The meeting goes to History with a timestamped transcript; Export Markdown… saves it. macOS asks once for System Audio Recording.
- **Live text with local Whisper.** The recorder shows the text while you speak (Live Text Display, on by default). What gets pasted is still the full transcription made when you let go.
- **Undo or rewrite the last paste by voice.** Say only "scratch that" or "删掉刚才那句" to remove what Yap pasted last, or use the Undo Last Paste shortcut. Rewrite Last Dictation: press, say how to change it ("make it more formal", "改正式一点"), press again, and the text is replaced in place. Both check first that the text is still where Yap put it and unchanged.
- **Text around the cursor as context.** Cleanup sees the app, window title and the text around where you're typing, so names and terms match. On by default per mode (Text Around the Cursor); password fields are never read.
- **Chinese cleanup without a model.** Even with no AI cleanup, 嗯 / 呃 and a leading 那个 are removed, spoken 换行 and 新段落 become line breaks, and Traditional becomes Simplified. Each is a switch under Chinese Cleanup.
- **Parakeet Ultra**, a more accurate Parakeet V3 (same languages, no Chinese), in the local models.

## For macOS 26

- Apple Intelligence can be used for cleanup, on the Mac, after turning on Models › Advanced. It stays behind that switch until it's measured against Yap Refine.

# Yap 1.3.0（中文）

1.2.0 之后的改动。

Yap 是一个开源（GPL-3.0）的 macOS 听写 app，fork 自 VoiceInk。按住快捷键，用中文、英文或者一句话里中英混着说，松开：Yap 转写、清理，然后粘贴。可以用本机模型、你自己的服务商 key，或者 Yap Cloud。

## 经过 Apple 签名和公证

Yap 现在用 Developer ID 签名并经过 Apple 公证，浏览器下载的 zip 打开时只会出现普通的“从互联网下载”确认，不会再被拦住。因为签名变了，这次更新后 macOS 会再要一次权限：

- 麦克风：下一次录音时重新允许。
- 辅助功能：“系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 辅助功能”里 Yap 看起来还开着，但粘贴不会生效，要先把 Yap 从列表里移除（−）再加回来，或者关掉再打开。
- 钥匙串：macOS 会问一次 Yap 能否使用已保存的 API key 或 Yap Cloud 登录信息，选“始终允许”。

之后的更新会保留这些权限。

## 从音频文件导出字幕

- 用本地 Whisper 模型转写音频或视频文件后，可以在转写页面或历史记录里导出字幕：SRT、WebVTT，或带时间戳的 Markdown。
- 时间戳按整个文件计算，分段解码的长文件、中途换语言的录音也对得上。

## 词典也用在云端转写上

- 用 OpenRouter 或 Yap Cloud 时，词典里的词现在会发给转写模型，而不只是给清理用，并且放在每个模型真正会读的字段里。在中英混说测试集上，默认模型（mai-transcribe-2）的关键词从 82 个里对 59 个提高到 68 个。
- gpt-4o-transcribe、gpt-4o-mini-transcribe 和 whisper-large-v3 也会收到词典。不理会词典的模型（qwen3-asr-flash、gemini）不发。
- 你选定的转写语言也会一起发；自动检测时不变。

## 主页、词典和设置

- 主页显示听写、撤销上次粘贴、改写上一次听写的快捷键，可以直接去设置里修改；统计数字改成了和页面其他部分一样的卡片。
- 词典列表可以只看自动添加或手动添加的词，点“选择”可以一次删掉多个。
- 撤销上次粘贴和改写上一次听写在设置里有了单独的“语音修改”分组。中文清理的四个选项改成开关，每个都带说明。
- 帮助 › 功能导览：每个功能一张卡片，写着当前快捷键，点“带我去”跳到设置它的地方。

## 1.2.0 里已经有、但当时的说明漏掉了

下面这些在 1.2.0 里就有，只是没写进说明：

- **会议录制。** 右 ⌘ + 空格（可以在 设置 › 快捷键 › 录制会议 里改）开始录麦克风和其他 app 的声音，再按一次结束。开会过程中 Yap 边录边转写，结束后用当前模式的 AI 服务商写纪要（摘要、决定、待办、未决问题）。会议保存在历史记录里，带时间戳的转写可以用“导出 Markdown…”保存。第一次使用时 macOS 会请求“系统音频录制”权限。
- **本地 Whisper 实时显示文字。** 说话时录音条上就能看到文字（实时文本显示，默认开启）。粘贴的仍然是松开后对整段录音的完整转写。
- **用语音撤销或改写上一次粘贴。** 只说“删掉刚才那句”或“scratch that”，就能删掉 Yap 上一次粘贴的文字，也可以用撤销上次粘贴的快捷键。改写上一次听写：按一下，说怎么改（“改正式一点”），再按一下，文字原地替换。两者都会先确认文字还在原处、没被改过。
- **光标附近的文字作为上下文。** 清理时会参考当前 app、窗口标题和你正在输入位置前后的文字，人名、术语更容易写对。每个模式默认开启（光标附近的文字）；密码框永远不读。
- **不用模型的中文清理。** 即使不开 AI 清理，也会去掉“嗯 / 呃”和句首的“那个”，把说出来的“换行”“新段落”变成真正的换行，繁体转简体。每一项都是“中文清理”里的一个开关。
- **Parakeet Ultra**：更准确的 Parakeet V3（语言相同，不支持中文），在本地模型里。
